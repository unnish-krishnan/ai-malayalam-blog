## 🔑 Magic Keywords — ഇവ ഏത് Prompt-ലും ചേർക്കൂ

**Camera:**
- `Canon 85mm f/1.4` — portrait lens look
- `shallow depth of field` — blurred background
- `shot on 35mm film` — vintage feel

**Lighting:**
- `golden hour light` — warm glow
- `Rembrandt lighting` — dramatic shadows
- `soft window light` — natural indoor

**Style:**
- `cinematic color grading` — movie look
- `photorealistic` — real photo look
- `hyperrealistic` — ultra real

**Mood:**
- `emotional expression` — feeling in eyes
- `moody atmosphere` — dark, dramatic
- `ethereal dreamy` — soft, magical
