## 🎯 ChatGPT-4o-ൽ Action Figure ഉണ്ടാക്കാൻ

ChatGPT-ൽ ഒരു selfie upload ചെയ്ത ശേഷം ഈ prompt കൊടുക്കൂ:

```

Label on the box: "[Your Name] — [Your Profession]"
Include small accessories related to their job.
Plastic toy style, glossy packaging, retail store quality.
White background, studio lighting.
```
