## ChatGPT-ൽ ചെയ്യൂ — Step by Step

### Step 1: ChatGPT-ൽ Photo Upload ചെയ്യൂ

ChatGPT → Plus/Free account → Image upload button → നിങ്ങളുടെ selfie/portrait upload

### Step 2: ഈ Prompt Copy ചെയ്‌ത് Paste ചെയ്യൂ

**Basic Indian 80s Portrait:**
```
Transform this photo into a 1980s Indian studio portrait. 
Style: Bollywood film poster look, warm golden-orange studio lighting, 
soft focus background, film grain texture. 
Add traditional Indian clothing if casual — saree or kurta. 
Big voluminous hair, bold kajal eyes, classic jewellery. 
Kodak film camera aesthetic, slightly faded colours.
```

**Vintage Family Photo Style:**
```
Convert this into a 1980s Kerala/South Indian family photograph.
Style: Old studio photography — wooden chair props, painted backdrop 
(garden or cityscape), fluorescent studio lighting turned warm.
Formal traditional dress — half saree, silk pavada, mundu.
Film grain, slight colour fade, polaroid-border crop.
```

**80s Bollywood Hero/Heroine Style:**
```

Male: Bell-bottom trousers, printed shirt, thick moustache, slicked-back hair.
Female: Chiffon saree, big bun with flowers, winged eyeliner.
Set: Colorful Holi or garden backdrop.
Camera: Analog film grain, desaturated highlights.
```
