## 1. Remove.bg — ഏറ്റവും മികച്ചത്

**URL**: remove.bg  
**Free**: 5 images/month (HD), unlimited (low-res)  
**Mobile App**: ✓ Available

**Steps:**
1. remove.bg open ചെയ്യുക
2. "Upload Image" click ചെയ്യുക
3. 5 seconds — background gone! ✓
4. Download ചെയ്യുക

**Best for**: People photos, product images

## 2. Adobe Express (Free)

**URL**: express.adobe.com  
**Free**: Unlimited with free account  

**Steps:**
1. Adobe Express → "Remove Background"
2. Photo upload ചെയ്യുക
3. Auto remove → Download

**Plus**: Background-ൽ color, gradient, image ചേർക്കാം

## 3. Canva — Social Media Ready

**URL**: canva.com  
**Free**: 5 uses/month (free plan)

**Steps:**
1. Canva → New Design → Upload photo
2. Photo select → Edit → "BG Remover"
3. Background remove → New background add ചെയ്യുക
4. Social media size-ൽ download
