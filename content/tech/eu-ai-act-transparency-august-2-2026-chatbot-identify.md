August 2 മുതൽ Europe-ൽ AI-ന് identify ചെയ്യൂ mandatory — world's biggest tech regulation live!

## EU AI Act — What Is It?

**European Union Artificial Intelligence Act** (2024):
- World's first comprehensive AI law
- Applies: All companies serving EU users
- Risk-based approach: High risk → more rules

**August 2, 2026**: Article 50 (Transparency obligations) live!

## 4 Key Transparency Rules — Now Mandatory

### Rule 1: AI Must Identify Itself

**Who**: Any AI system directly interacting with humans (chatbots, virtual assistants)

**What**: Must **clearly disclose** it is an AI

**Examples:**
- Customer service chatbot: "Hi, I'm an AI assistant"
- WhatsApp Meta AI: Must say "I am an AI" if asked
- Claude, ChatGPT: Already compliant (mostly)
- **Problem**: Bots posing as humans → now illegal in EU

**Exception**: Authorized law enforcement, obvious artistic context

### Rule 2: AI-Generated Content Disclosure

**Who**: Any provider creating AI-generated content for public

**What**: Must label content as AI-generated

**Examples:**
- AI-written news articles → "Generated by AI" label
- AI social media posts → disclosure
- AI marketing emails → disclose
- YouTube videos (AI voice) → label

**Instagram/TikTok impact**: AI-generated videos must show label

### Rule 3: Emotion Recognition Rules

**Who**: Companies using emotion recognition AI

**What**: Must inform users when emotion AI active

**Examples:**
- Job interview AI (analyzing facial expressions): Must disclose
- Retail stores (customer mood tracking): Inform customers
- Call centers (voice emotion analysis): Tell callers

**Kerala companies**: BPO/call centers using emotion AI → EU clients → compliance needed!

### Rule 4: Deepfake Disclosure

**Who**: Anyone creating deepfakes or AI-generated images of real people

**What**: Clearly mark as synthetic/AI-generated

**Critical**: **Public interest content** (news, politics, satire) → extra strict rules

**Examples:**
- AI face swap videos → "Deepfake" label mandatory
- AI politician images → label
- Satire OK → but must be clearly labeled satire

## Penalties

| Violation | Fine |
|-----------|------|
| Transparency rule violation | **€15 million** or **3% global revenue** |
| Higher risk violations | €30 million or 6% revenue |
| Wrong info to regulators | €7.5 million or 1.5% revenue |

**For big tech:**
- Meta (EU revenue ~€15B): 3% = **€450 million** fine risk
- Google: 3% = **€2+ billion** fine risk
