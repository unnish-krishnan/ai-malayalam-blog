Android phone-ൽ Claude-ന് Google Assistant-ൻ്റ് power കിട്ടും — EU ഒരു historic order ഇട്ടു!

## EU Order — What Exactly?

**European Commission** → Google → Digital Markets Act (DMA) enforcement:

**Google must do by July 2027:**

1. **Voice Activation**: "Hey Claude" / "Hey ChatGPT" — Android phones-ൽ default-ആക്കൂ option
2. **Screen Context**: Third-party AI → screen-ൽ ഉള്ളത് read ചെയ്‌ത് assist
3. **App Actions**: AI assistant → apps control ചെയ്‌ത് tasks complete

**Currently Google blocks these** — only Google Assistant gets deep Android access.

## DMA — Digital Markets Act എന്താണ്?

EU-ൻ്റ് tech giant regulation law (2022):

**"Gatekeepers"** (designated companies):
- Google, Apple, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, TikTok

**DMA rules:**
- Interoperability mandatory
- Self-preferencing banned
- Third-party access required

**Penalty**: Global revenue 10% (Google: ~$35 billion fine possible)
