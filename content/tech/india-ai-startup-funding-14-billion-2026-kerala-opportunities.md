## How to Win Funding — 2026 Guide

### For First-time Founders

**Step 1: Proof before pitch**
```
Idea → Build MVP → Get 10 paying customers
→ Show ₹X revenue → Then pitch investors
```

**Step 2: Defensible data/IP**
- What unique data do you have?
- Why can't a big company copy this in 6 months?
- Patent possible? Data moat? Network effect?

**Step 3: Right investors**
- KSUM grants: ₹5-25 lakh early stage
- Indian Angel Network: ₹25L-2Cr seed
- Sequoia Surge: India-Southeast Asia focused
- Peak XV (formerly Sequoia India): Series A+

### AI Startup Specific

**What investors want in AI:**
- Proprietary training data (not just OpenAI wrapper)
- Domain expertise moat
- Clear enterprise customer
- Revenue > $10K/month before Series A

**Red flags:**
- "We use GPT-4 API" — easily replaceable
- No paying customers
- Technical team only, no sales person
