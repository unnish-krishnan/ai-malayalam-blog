## Tesla vs Waymo — Two Approaches

### Tesla-ൻ്റ് Strategy: "Move Fast"

### Waymo-ൻ്റ് Strategy: "Move Safe"

## India-ൽ Self-Driving Cars — When?

India challenges unique:
- **Traffic chaos**: Cows, autos, bikes, pedestrians — unpredictable
- **Road quality**: Potholes, unmarked lanes
- **Regulations**: Motor Vehicles Act not ready for autonomous
- **Infrastructure**: GPS mapping incomplete in rural areas

**Timeline prediction:**
- **2026-2027**: Controlled pilots in Bangalore, Mumbai
- **2028-2030**: Limited commercial autonomous taxis
- **2030+**: Broader deployment

**Kerala specific**: Kochi smart city project-ൽ autonomous shuttle pilot plans.
