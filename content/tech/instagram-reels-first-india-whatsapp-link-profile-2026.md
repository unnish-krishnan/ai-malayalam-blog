## 2. WhatsApp Link — Instagram Profile-ൽ!

Instagram profile-ൽ ഇനി **WhatsApp number directly link** ചെയ്യൂ:

1. Instagram → Profile → Edit Profile
2. **"Contact options"** → "WhatsApp"

### Profile-ൽ കാണുന്നത്:
- "WhatsApp" button — followers direct message!
- Phone dialer open ആകാതെ WhatsApp ആകും
- Business accounts-ന് especially powerful

### Kerala Business Owners-ന്:

**Before**: DM → slow response, easy to miss
**Now**: WhatsApp → instant chat, familiar platform
