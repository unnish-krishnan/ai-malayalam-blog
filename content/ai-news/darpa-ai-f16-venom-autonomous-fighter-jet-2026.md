## DARPA VENOM — What Happened

**July 2026** — Eglin Air Force Base, Florida:

- 3 F-16 fighter jets modified with AI kit
- July: First autonomous AI flight completed
- Human pilot: Cockpit-ൽ ഉണ്ടായിരുന്നു — supervisor only
