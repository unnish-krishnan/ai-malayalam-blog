## Meta's Business Model Shift

**Why Meta doing this:**

### Revenue Diversification
- Ads: 97% revenue (risky — iOS privacy changes hurt)
- Subscription: Stable, predictable
- AI features: Expensive → must monetize

### Competitive Pressure
- ChatGPT Plus: 100M paying users
- Apple One: Bundle success
- Meta: "We must also have paid tier"

### AI Cost Reality
- Meta AI: Costs per query (GPU time)
- Llama inference: Not free
- 3 billion users × AI = massive cost
- Free forever? Unsustainable

## Ad-Free Option

**Controversial add-on:**
- Instagram ad-free: Additional cost
- EU regulation forcing this (DMA)
- India: Ads continue for free users

**Kerala reality**: Most users tolerate ads → free tier dominant

## Privacy With Paid Tier

**Important caveat:**
- Paid tier: **Still** data collected
- "Ad-free" ≠ "data-free"
- Meta's core business: Data monetization continues
- Even paid users: Data used for AI training (unless opted out)

**Setting to check:**
Settings → Privacy → AI Training → Opt out
