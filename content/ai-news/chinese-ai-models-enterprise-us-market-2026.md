## Should India Developers Use Chinese AI?

### Advantages ✅
- **Cost**: 60-80% cheaper
- **Performance**: 90-95% of frontier quality for most tasks
- **Open-source options**: DeepSeek MIT license — self-host possible
- **Multilingual**: Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam support growing

### Concerns ⚠️

**Data Privacy:**
- Chinese models → data China servers-ൽ
- GDPR/India DPDP Act compliance unclear
- Customer data, proprietary code share ചെയ്യരുത്

**US Export Controls:**
- Some Chinese models restricted categories-ൽ
- Business clients US-based ആണെങ്കിൽ compliance risk

**Reliability:**
- Service interruptions — US-China tension time-ൽ
- Support quality — Western models better

### Kerala IT Companies — Guidance

**Safe to use Chinese models:**
- Internal tools (not customer data)
- Research, exploration
- Personal projects
- Open-source self-hosted versions

**Avoid Chinese models:**
- Client data processing
- Financial/medical information
- US/EU customer-facing products
- Sensitive business information
