## Discovery Loop — How It Works

**Current science problem:**
- Human researcher: 1-2 experiments per week
- Hypothesis → Test → Analyze → New hypothesis: Months/years
- Drug discovery: Average 12 years, $2.6 billion per drug

**Discovery Loop approach:**
```
AI → Hypothesis generate (thousands simultaneously)
AI → Experiment design ചെയ്യൂ
Robots → Experiments run ചെയ്യൂ (automated labs)
AI → Results analyze ചെയ്ത് next hypothesis generate
Loop → Continuously iterate
```

**Goal**: Science speed 10-100x faster!
