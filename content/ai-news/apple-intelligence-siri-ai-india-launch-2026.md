## Key Features — India Relevant

### 1. Priority Notifications

**Problem**: 200+ notifications/day → mental load

```

🔴 Priority: "Mom calling 3rd time" (repeated call)
🟡 Normal: "Your order shipped"

```

**Kerala users**: Office WhatsApp + family group + news apps → AI filter helps!

### 2. Writing Tools — Everywhere

In any text field (iMessage, Gmail, Instagram DM, Notes):
- **Proofread**: Grammar, spell check

**Work use case**: Email to boss — Siri AI formal version suggest ചെയ്‌ത്!

