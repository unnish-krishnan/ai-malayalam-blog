## What is MoE? (Mixture of Experts)

**2.4T parameters, but only 95B active:**

Traditional model: All parameters activate always
**MoE model**: 2.4T parameters → 95B experts per query

**Like a hospital:**
- 500 specialists available
- Each patient: See only relevant specialists (4-5)
- Efficiency: High, Cost: Low, Quality: Specialist-level

**Result**: 2.4T model quality at 95B model cost!
